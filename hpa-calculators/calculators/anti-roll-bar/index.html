Assumptions and limits
  • A U-shaped bar of round section, with two straight lever arms in bending and a straight torsion bar between them. Blade-type and splined-lever bars are not covered.
  • Bushing and drop-link compliance are ignored. On a road car they can be a large share of the real rate.
  • Stiffness is at the lever arm tip. Turning it into roll stiffness needs track width and the drop-link motion ratio.
  • Bends are treated as sharp corners, so a real bar is slightly softer than this.
  • Steel is taken as E = 200 GPa and G = 78 GPa, chromoly as 205 and 80. Heat treatment changes strength, not stiffness.
