Assumptions and limits
  • Ride frequency is the undamped natural frequency of one corner on its spring. Dampers and tyre stiffness are not included, so the real car sits a little above these figures.
  • Unsprung mass is per corner, not per axle.
  • Motion ratio is wheel travel divided by spring travel, taken as constant. On most suspensions it changes through the travel.
  • Each axle is treated as symmetrical, at half the axle mass per corner. Use corner weights directly if your car is not.
  • Spring rate is the spring alone. Anti-roll bars, bump rubbers and helper springs all add to the rate the wheel sees.
  • Tyre vertical stiffness is in series with the spring and is ignored. It matters most at high frequencies.
