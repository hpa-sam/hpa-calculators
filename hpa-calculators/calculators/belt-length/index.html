Assumptions and limits
  • Length is the theoretical belt path. Buy the nearest size up and set tension with the centre distance.
  • An open two-pulley drive with both pulleys in the same plane. Idlers, tensioners and crossed belts are not covered.
  • Diameters are pitch diameters, where the belt actually sits. Outer flange diameters overstate the length.
  • No belt slip is assumed. Surface speed is at the drive pulley.
  • Sizing suggestions are exact diameters. Real pulleys come in fixed sizes, so pick the nearest and re-check the speeds.
  • Belt power capacity, tension and wrap angle come from the manufacturer and are not checked here.
